## English

First public release of ANMP, the message format and client core of anynear. The protocol version is v1; the library
version starts at 0.9.0.

### Specification
- `spec/anmp.md` and `spec/anmp.cddl` describe protocol version v1 as the anynear apps use it.
- New section "Security considerations": goals, limits (no forward secrecy, the invitation link is the circle secret, weaker sender proof over radio, random nonces under long-lived keys) and what relays, radio listeners and push services see.
- Header field 8 (`askTags`) is reserved: a v1 `ask` does not carry it, readers still parse it.
- The example of the radio list uses an invented community.
- Everything to a person goes once to its mailbox recipient (field 7 of the device list) instead of once per device; every device of the person reads it, also one back from the emergency card.
- Packets to a person's mailbox recipient carry no signature: the pair key proves the sending device, which must be listed with exactly its keys in the sender's chain. Signatures stay where many share a key.
- Over radio, direct packets go to the person as well: route tag and pair key of its mailbox recipient, taken by every device of the person in range; pickups include the person.
- Content field 37 `author`: own copies of received direct packets name their author. Packets heard only over radio and, once after the update, the old per-device entries of the own mailbox are written as such copies, so every own device and the emergency card get them.
- Content field 38 `passedOn`: a circle or direct message that passes on one the sender got carries `true`, without any origin; not over radio.
- Device entry field 5 `moved`: a device notes in the device list that it moved its old entries and history to the person.
- `sync-contact` field 5 `since`: when the contact was added on the sending device (ms), so another own device keeps the same order.
- New "Device space": settings of each own device, so that a phone replacing another one can take them over.
- `sync` field 7: the grants of the published contact links go to the own other devices, so whoever writes through such a link is a contact at once on every device of the person; withdrawing the link leaves a tombstone `link-revoked:<grant hash>`.
- `alive` field 12 `position`: where the sender's phone is (latitude and longitude in degrees × 10 000, time located, accuracy), only in a sign of life nobody confirmed (state 4) and only if the person allowed it; a newer packet without it removes it at the readers. Field 11 is reserved.
- `alive` field 13 `guard` (ADR 0083 §5.7): with every sign of life the phone leaves a finished alarm at the relays, kept back until the helpers' deadline (ANDP extension `not_before`). The next sign of life deletes it; if the phone goes silent, the relays release it and wake the helpers, with the place of the phone if the person allowed it. Readers drop it if they know a newer packet of the plan.
- New kind 30 `position` with content field 42 `place`: what a person shares of its place with one other person (level, a point or an area, until when, or the request to be shown the reader's place). It goes to the person like a direct message, never into a circle space, and not over radio.

### Libraries
- `core`: a call for help that arrives before the person of the sending device is known shows under that person as soon as it is known; a new picture from another own device is fetched again if it was not readable yet.
- `core`: a place message without a point (the phone could not locate itself) keeps the last known point while the level stays the same; a different level still ends it (`PlaceKeeping`).
- Kotlin Multiplatform: `format` (packets, encryption, signatures, key derivation; no dependencies) and `core` (circles, contacts, mailboxes, sending and fetching on ANDP).
- `pendingEmergencyCard()`: the code of an emergency card made but not yet confirmed, so the app can show it again instead of making a new card.
- Go: the radio part of the format.
- `core`: in a circle history, messages of a removed device stay with their person if they were written before it was removed (`personOfDevice` for messages an app already keeps).
- `core`: a contact whose mailbox refuses every direct message with 403 (for example after the person deleted everything) is marked unreachable until something of the person arrives again.
- `core`: removing a contact revokes the grant given to its mailbox recipient as well, not only the ones of its devices. If a mailbox refuses a revoked grant while its owner has given a newer one since (the contact was added again), the newer one is used and the message tried again.
- `core`: a grant issued under a circle purpose and a contact purpose at once (possible before the fix for unique grants) is revoked under both when the circle revokes it, also in a state an older version stored; the contact gets a new grant right away and can write again. Contact grants are renewed per mailbox recipient.
- `core`: `undeliveredWrites()` counts writes no relay has yet; deleting everything waits for those instead of for every holder.
- Radio list: every region names its LoRa region (`lora`) and optionally a frequency; `regionFor` picks the region for a radio with 2 km of hysteresis at borders (Kotlin and Go). `core.radio`: `RadioTarget` carries the settings of an area into radio profile, gateway plan and Wi-Fi setup; `isAreaChange` tells a mere change of area; the web facade offers `radioAreas`, `radioAreaAt` and `radioArea`.
- `core`: `sendCircle` and `sendDirect` take `passedOn` (also the web facade), `CoreEvent.Message` reports it.
- `core`: after agreeing to a contact request from an introduction, `canWriteTo` and sending work at once, before the person's key chain arrives.
- `core`: `MemberState.addedAt` tells when a contact was added (ms; 0 for contacts added before this version), also in the web facade. Apps sort contacts without messages by it.
- `core`: an introduction keeps its card (`CoreEvent.Message.intro`) on every way a direct message arrives: own copies for the other own devices and for a device added later, forwarded radio packets, messages of a request and the wake-up fetch.
- Test vectors in `vectors/`; Kotlin writes them, Go checks them.
- Radio flow vectors (`vectors/anmp-v1-radio-flows.json`): the patron's behaviour step by step, with the settings of the app and of the bridge; Kotlin writes them through `RadioRelay`, Go plays them.
- Go: the patron role (`Patron`, `AppPatron`, `BridgePatron`) for the bridge: it waits 30 s plus a random part, stays quiet if another patron answers, offers a one-time key, opens the pickup and sends from the radio inbox within the limits (5 per ask, 20 per hour and patronage, 60 per hour and gateway). With the gateway's budget used up it does not offer itself, and it sends a packet to the same asker at most once an hour.
- `core`: a patron's hop limit is the measured hops, at least 1; the hourly limit counts per patronage and per gateway (the app has one, its radio, so it stays 20 per hour). With the budget used up the app does not offer itself as patron, and it sends a packet to the same asker at most once an hour, also after a new ask.
- `core`: `aliveSetPosition` hands in the last place the phone knows for the own sign of life; `AlivePlan.position` gives the helpers' apps the place of a sign of life nobody confirmed. Over radio the label stays behind first, then the place. Go: `Alive.Position`. New vector `radio.aliveMissedPacket`.
- New kind 31 `sos` with content field 44: a person calls its helpers, into circles (also over radio) and to single persons, with place, battery and a trail; helpers answer with state 3, state 2 is the all-clear. `core`: `raiseSos`, `updateSos`, `endSos`, `careSos`, `sosCalls`, event `SosChanged`. Go knows kind 31.
- Radio: packets have an internal order (`RadioPriority`): a call for the helpers goes before every queued message and waits for a radio that is away; a place waits while a message waits.
- Radio: kind `position` goes over radio, per person at most every 15 minutes and at once for a new level, a request or an answer (`RadioWay.place`); field 43 `spot` goes along in radio messages if it fits (`radioTextPacket`, `queueRadioText` take `spot`, `CoreEvent.RadioMessage.spot` delivers it).
- Field 42 `place` may ride along with a direct message, over the internet and over radio, instead of a packet of kind position (`sendDirect`, `queueRadioText`, `radioTextPacket` take `place: PlaceShare`); never in a circle message.
- `sos`: a helper's device confirms a call with a receipt, over the internet and over radio; `SosCall.arrived` names the persons it arrived with.
- `meta` fields 6 to 8: the places of a circle as one list with its time and device (`setCirclePlaces`, `circlePlaces`, `CoreEvent.CirclePlacesChanged`); older apps skip them.
- `sos` states 4 to 6: being a contact for someone's calls needs a yes, once (`askSosContacts`, `answerSosAsk`, `sosContacts`, `sosAsks`, `sosAgreed`, events `SosAsked`, `SosContactsChanged`). The reader decides: a call from someone it did not say yes to must not ring.
- `sos` state 8 with field 7 `precise`: the asker tells each contact that said yes whether its calls carry its precise place (`setSosPrecise`, `sosPreciseOf`, also in the web facade). New vector `cbor.contentSosReady`.
- `place` fields 5 `label` and 6 `since`: the name the person gave the place it is at and since when (`sharePlace` takes `label`, `sinceMillis`; `PersonPlace.label`, `since`).
- `spot` kind 4 with field 3 `until`: the invitation to everyone in a circle to share their place for a time (`MessageSpot.until`).
- `core`: `AlivePlan.guard` tells a helper's app that the relays reported the phone as silent; `AliveLogic.status` then gives the alarm of the deadline, once. `CoreTimings.aliveGuardEarly`. New vector `radio.aliveGuard`.
- Content field 43 `spot` in circle and direct messages: a meeting point, the question where the reader is, or the sender's place as the answer; `sendCircle` and `sendDirect` take it, `CoreEvent.Message.spot` delivers it.
- `core`: `mapLists` reads the root lists of a published map space, `mapPackage` an object (the list of a region, a tile) from the space of the map data (`AnmpCoreConfig.mapSpace`, `mapGrant`, `mapDataSpace`, `mapDataGrant`; not set up by default).
- `core`: `sharePlace`, `askPlace`, `places` and `forgetPlace`, with the events `PlaceChanged` and `PlaceAsked`. Go knows kind 30.
- A sign of life nobody confirmed is a call for the helpers: `sos` field 6 `plan`, the call's ID is `AnmpSos.aliveId` of plan and `due` (Go: `AliveSosID`), and the alarm a phone leaves at the relays carries the call in field 44. `core`: the phone raises the call when the helpers' alarm is due, a helper opens the same call by its own deadline (`aliveTick`), `SosCall.plan` and `reason`, `aliveCallId`, `aliveSetPosition` takes a trail, `CoreEvent.AliveWake.call`. New vectors `cbor.contentSosAlive`, `radio.aliveSos`.
- `alive` field 14 `calls`: in a packet that says nobody confirmed and in the alarm left at the relays, the phone says that it raises the call itself. `core`: `wakeFetch` reports calls for the helpers as `CoreEvent.SosWake`, so a notification extension rings for the call and not for the packet before it.
- A sign of life may go to one person instead of a circle, like a direct message: kinds 27 and 28 also between a device pair, over the internet and over radio. Its alarm for the relays lies in a space of the plan's own, derived from `alive` field 15 `watch` like a circle space. A call (kind 31) for a single person goes over radio, too. `core`: `startAlive(person = …)`, `AlivePlan.peer`. New vector `radio.aliveWatch`.
- `core`: login with anynear (ADR 0122): `parseLoginLink` reads `https://anynear.app/l#…` (also on anynear.net) only for the issuers in `AnmpCoreConfig.loginIssuers` (default `https://id.anynear.app`), `loginInfo` fetches service, domain and the three numbers, `confirmLogin` signs with the device key and sends the confirmation with the own proof (`LoginResult` tells ok, wrong number, expired and the rest). `AnmpLogin.confirmBytes` gives the same bytes as ANIP's `PersonConfirmBytes`. The web facade offers the same.

### Compatibility
- Version v1 constants never change. An incompatible change will be `anmp/v2` next to v1.
- The Kotlin core needs ANDP 0.9.0 checked out next to this repository.
